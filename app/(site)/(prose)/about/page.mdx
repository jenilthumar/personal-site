# About

I'm Jenil — a product and visual designer. I care deeply about creating
functional and visually clean, strong digital experiences and products: how
they look, how they feel, and how they behave.

I'm currently designing at [Roboto Studio](https://www.robotostudio.com), where
I work across brand, product, and the web — from first principles to the last
pixel.

## How I work

I like to start with the problem, not the interface. The best work tends to come
from understanding the people on the other side of the screen, then removing
everything that isn't helping them.

- Product and interface design, end to end
- Design systems that teams can actually maintain
- Art direction for the web

## Beyond the screen

When I'm not designing, I'm usually outdoors with a camera — most of the
photography on this site comes from trips into the mountains.
