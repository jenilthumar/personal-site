Wheatsville is a community-owned grocery co-op. The site needed to feel as
welcoming as the store: human, a little playful, and easy for thousands of
member-owners to use.

## The approach

We leaned into the co-op's character — generous color, hand-set headlines, and
photography of real people and real food — while keeping the shopping and
membership flows simple and quick.

- Brand refresh for digital
- Member-focused information architecture
- An accessible, easy-to-maintain design system

## Outcome

A site that feels like the neighborhood it serves.
