Sitenote is a site-management tool for teams who spend their day on the ground,
not at a desk. The challenge was density without clutter — surfacing the right
information for the right person at the right moment.

## The approach

We started in the field, shadowing site engineers to understand how decisions
actually get made. The product follows that reality: glanceable status, fast
capture, and a structure that holds up on a phone in bright sun.

- Information architecture and core flows
- A component system built for dense, data-heavy views
- Design for offline-first, low-bandwidth conditions

## Outcome

Less time chasing updates, more time building.
