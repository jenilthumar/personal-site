Opera Group is a real-estate developer building considered residential and
commercial spaces. The brief was a website that feels as composed as the
architecture itself: quiet typography, generous space, and photography that
leads.

## The approach

We treated the site like a building — structure first, ornament never. A
restrained grid lets each project breathe, while a warm neutral palette keeps
attention on the work.

- Art direction and visual system
- Responsive web design, end to end
- A flexible project template for future launches

## Outcome

A site that loads fast, reads clearly, and gives every development the room to
stand on its own.
