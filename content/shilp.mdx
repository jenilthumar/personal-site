Shilp is a design-and-make studio working at the edge of craft and technology.
The site needed to feel tactile — like you could reach in and pick something up.

## The approach

We built the experience around material: large, slow-moving 3D renders, close
crops of texture, and type that gets out of the way. Motion is used sparingly,
only where it adds a sense of weight.

- Visual direction and motion language
- Real-time 3D integrated into the page
- A modular case-study layout

## Outcome

A portfolio that feels as considered as the objects it showcases.
