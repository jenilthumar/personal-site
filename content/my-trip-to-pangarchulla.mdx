Pangarchulla is a steep, snow-laden summit in the Garhwal Himalayas. We started
before sunrise, headlamps tracing the ridge, and reached the top as the light
turned everything gold.

A small set of photographs from the way up — frozen streams, long shadows, and
a view that made the early start worth it.
